# Heritable variation: capability gate

14 October 2026. Static mechanism assessment following the 24-trajectory seeded copying recheck. No new simulation result is claimed.

Subsequent resolution: opt-in v3 now permits one mismatched endpoint at an attenuated local catalytic rate. The [completed capability campaign](../research/variation/REPORT.md) records 12 replayed trajectories, 312 changed-signature births and 279 faithful altered-parent births across four active runs. The v2 restriction described below remains true. Competitive selection and adaptation remain unproven.

## Finding

The demonstrated v2 dimer lineage cannot generate a changed material-pair signature through its event-attributed copying mechanism. This is a restriction of that mechanism and experiment, not a proof that every configuration of Genesis forbids every form of inherited variation.

## Argument from the existing laws

In `src/matter/transitions.rs`, `contact_source` accepts a source edge A-B for reactants P-Q only when A and P have identical materials and B and Q have identical materials. Binding creates the P-Q bond without altering either material. Thus every contact-attributed child has exactly its parent's ordered material pair; canonicalizing reversal preserves equality. Induction gives the same signature at every catalytic generation.

In `src/matter/state.rs`, particle material is immutable and checked against its initial identity. Mutation of a molecular composition would need recruitment of different atoms, not changing elemental identity.

The demonstrated `examples/material_autocatalysis.rs` condition sets ordinary binding, breaking, conformation switching and coupling to zero. Valence is one, every particle starts active, all ports are open and every affinity is equal. Consequently this condition supplies no demonstrated alternative inheritable conformation, branching structure or competing compositional variant. Motion and energy changes alone do not establish inheritance.

The observer also rejects a product with a different signature. Any future variation assay must record changed products rather than reuse that fidelity assertion. Updating the observer alone cannot overcome the exact-match physical rule.

## Consequence for the next experiment

Two preseeded signatures sharing an elemental resource could establish differential amplification and sorting among supplied variants. That would be useful selection evidence if causally controlled, but would not establish new variation arising within a lineage. The previous two variants used disjoint material pairs in separate worlds and therefore did not measure competition.

Do not run the unchanged copying experiment longer expecting material-pair mutation. Do not claim adaptation from random differences in population size.

## Smallest proposed research change

Investigate a separately versioned local interaction law with graded source-reactant compatibility, allowing a small, explicitly bounded off-signature catalytic rate while retaining a preference for matching contacts. This would permit recruitment of alternative atoms under local energy, adjacency, port and conservation constraints. It would be an engineered artificial-chemistry hypothesis, not an emergent law or a guaranteed route to life. Existing v1/v2 semantics and archives must remain unchanged.

Before implementation, specify the compatibility table and rate calculation, identify a viable altered product able to catalyze its own signature, and state resource-sharing conditions. A convincing experiment must establish: a changed child born through recorded parent catalysis; reproduction of that child's changed signature; inherited differences in reproductive success under competition; inactive-source and copying-disabled controls; independently selected seeds; and no observer-driven fitness or physical transitions. Distinguish recurrent production of the same error from a mutant lineage copying itself.

This proposal does not establish open-ended evolution: dimers have only ten unordered material-pair signatures. Larger compositional information carriers remain a separate capability question.

## Status

Seeded copying capability is supported by the previous recheck. Within-lineage material-pair variation is excluded by the demonstrated mechanism. General heritable variation, competitive selection and adaptive evolution remain unproven. No laws were modified and no simulations were run for this assessment.
