# Material v3: within-lineage variation capability

Prospective protocol, 14 October 2026. This introduces an explicitly engineered artificial law, not a claim that copying errors arise under v2 or that realistic chemistry is simulated.

V1 has no edge-specific contact catalysis. V2 requires both reactant materials to match their adjacent source edge. V3 uses the same local geometry, active source states, source bond, valence, ports, affinity, excitation cost, conflict resolution and provenance. An exact match receives the existing bonus. One mismatched endpoint receives that bonus divided by 16 using integer arithmetic; two mismatches receive no contact bonus. The eligible source with greatest effective strength wins, then the existing source-ID ordering breaks ties. Material identities are unchanged; alternative environmental atoms are recruited. Ordinary binding remains governed by its existing separate rate.

V1 and v2 remain selectable and retain their historical semantics and checkpoint layouts. V3 has a distinct semantics label and checkpoint schema value. The default remains v2. The attenuation factor is part of v3's fixed laws, not tuned during a recorded experiment.

Run the existing seeded-copying geometry with two initial pairs (0,1) and (2,3), fresh seeds 41 and 42, and three conditions: active v3, inactive-source v3 and active v2. Twelve trajectories each run 2,048 ticks with 350 particles in an 8-cubed torus. Feedstock is initially energized and active, ordinary binding and breaking are disabled, and conformation switching is disabled. No interventions follow initialization. These favorable conditions establish mechanism capability only.

Before the run, each active condition must produce at least eight distinct persistent products, three catalytic generations and one descendant-mediated birth whose source and product atoms are all farther than five toroidal steps from the original template atoms at birth. Each active v3 trajectory must additionally produce at least one canonical material-pair change relative to its immediate parent and at least one faithful child of a parent whose signature differs from the original template. Active v2 must show zero changes and zero faithful altered-parent births; inactive-source v3 must produce zero products. Every trajectory must replay with complete event provenance and preserve all counted product bonds. Failures and ineligible trajectories are retained without relaxing thresholds.

The observer infers ancestry exclusively from accepted contact-catalysis events. It checks unique product IDs, permitted endpoint mismatch counts and exact signatures of immediate parent and product. Faithful mutant births require an altered parent copying its own signature, rather than the original ancestor independently generating the same copying error again. Energy is validated by the authoritative engine throughout. No fitness field, organism action or observer-selected reaction is introduced.

This is not a competition experiment, does not measure adaptive benefit and cannot establish open-ended evolution. Dimers have only ten unordered signatures; all catalytic strengths and affinities are equal here. Fresh seeds are new relative to the preceding capability recheck but there is no held-out confirmation cohort in this small capability assay. Distance-at-birth is not complete physical isolation.

Execute with `cargo build --offline --locked --release --bins --example material_variation --target-dir target/research-gate`, followed by the example executable and a new output directory.
