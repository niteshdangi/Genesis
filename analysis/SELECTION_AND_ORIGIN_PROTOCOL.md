# Shared-resource selection and unseeded material origin

Prospective research campaign, 14 October 2026. Uses existing v2/v3 laws; no new physical transitions or organism fields are added.

## Selection

Two deliberately seeded dimers, signatures (0,1) and (0,3), share the finite pool of material 0. Each begins with one active source bond in the same 350-particle, 8-cubed world. All particles are initially active and energized. Ordinary binding, breaking and switching are disabled. V2 exact-match copying preserves supplied signatures. Material 0 has zero catalytic strength. In favor-01 conditions material 1 has strength three and material 3 strength one; favor-03 reverses this. Neutral conditions set both to one. These are supplied inheritable chemical differences, not traits evolved during the run. No fitness reward is defined.

Four independent seeds 61-64, two swapped placements and four conditions (favor-01, favor-03, neutral, inactive-source) give 32 trajectories of 768 ticks. Every active lineage must reach depth three; every inactive-source control must have zero catalytic births. For each seed, the mean across placements of lineage-01 births minus lineage-03 births must be positive under favor-01, negative under favor-03, and differ by at least four births between those conditions. Neutral contrasts are reported descriptively, not required to equal zero. These small prospective criteria establish conditional differential amplification of supplied traits if passed, not population adaptation or statistical proof of open-ended evolution.

## Unseeded origin

Fresh seeds 81-84 start with all particles unbonded and inactive, ordinary binding rate 250 per million, switching rate 1,000 per million, breaking rate 100 per million and motion rate 100,000 per million. Initial excitation and the fixed reservoir provide environmental energy. Each seed has v3 and v1-disabled conditions, for eight trajectories of 2,048 ticks. No later intervention or template insertion occurs.

Ordinary bindings establish natural lineage roots. Accepted contact-catalysis events assign descendant ancestry; source bonds must exist in the preceding stage snapshot. Breaking removes the extant bond identity so recycled atoms cannot inherit a lineage through an unrelated new bond. All catalytic births, ordinary nucleations, signature changes, copying of descendant signatures, maximum catalytic depth and final extant lineages are recorded.

The prospective origin gate requires every v3 seed to produce at least ten catalytic births through at least three catalytic generations, and more catalytic births than its paired v1 control. All trajectories replay with full provenance. V1 is a mechanism-disabled control, not an identical microscopic trajectory.

This gate concerns spontaneous material autocatalysis. It cannot establish spontaneous life: ecological maintenance, damage repair, renewable material resources and sustained adaptation are not demonstrated. Finite dimers have only ten unordered material signatures and therefore cannot support indefinitely expanding inherited signature complexity. Intelligence and civilization cannot be inferred from this assay.

Both campaigns execute sequentially using the existing archive/observer machinery. Scientific negative results are saved and do not cancel the subsequent campaign. Execution or integrity failures stop the executable with preserved evidence. Thresholds are not relaxed after observing outcomes.
