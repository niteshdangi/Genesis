# Published research snapshots

These compact files capture the stated criteria and aggregate results from the final variation, competition, and unseeded autocatalysis campaigns. The matching Rust examples and research protocols are in `examples/` and `analysis/`.

The detailed per-tick events, native checkpoints, SQLite catalogs, and executable copies remain in local `runs/` directories ignored by Git. The published summaries are evidence of the completed local campaigns, not a substitute for their complete archives or independent reproduction. Run the examples to generate new archives. Original thresholds and negative outcomes are retained.

The earlier v0.7.0 roadmap refers to other local `runs/` paths that are not included here.
