# Selection and unseeded autocatalysis campaign

14 October 2026. Forty trajectories completed and replayed: 32 shared-resource competition trajectories and eight unseeded origin trajectories. Every final energy balance passed and event provenance remained complete. Existing v2/v3 physical laws were used; no new transitions or intelligence agents were added.

## Unseeded origin: prospective autocatalysis gate passed

All initial particles were unbonded and inactive. No template was inserted and no intervention occurred after initialization. Environmental energy, rare ordinary binding, conformation switching and ordinary motion enabled naturally formed active source bonds to catalyze further bonds under v3.

The four v1 mechanism-disabled controls produced zero contact-catalytic births. They could still form ordinary bonds; zero catalysis does not mean zero chemistry. All four v3 worlds exceeded the predeclared ten-birth, three-generation threshold. Total v3 catalytic births were 333. The observer removes a broken bond's ancestry before assigning new births and records immediate-parent signatures and depths.

This establishes spontaneous emergence of autocatalytic material lineages under designed artificial rules and initial/environmental conditions. It is not spontaneous life: an independently maintained organism, renewable material metabolism, repair, adaptation and ecological persistence are not established. The mechanism itself is intentionally encoded in v3. No claim of novel scientific discovery follows from these results.

## Competition: differential amplification observed; strict full gate failed

Two supplied signatures shared material 0. Initial positions were swapped, and the relative catalytic strengths of materials 1 and 3 were reversed. The following are placement-averaged differences in lineage-01 births minus lineage-03 births:

| Seed | Favor 01 | Favor 03 | Neutral |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 61 | 6.5 | -10 | 3 |
| 62 | 42 | -19 | 4 |
| 63 | 42.5 | -32 | 21.5 |
| 64 | 14.5 | -13.5 | -6 |

Every seed passed the prospectively stated directional reversal contrast. All eight inactive-source controls produced no catalytic descendants. Placement and stochastic effects remain substantial; neutral runs did not necessarily balance.

The full gate also required both lineages to attain three generations in every active trajectory. In seed 63's two favor-01 placements, the disadvantaged lineage reached only depth one. The recorded full selection gate is therefore **false**. Do not retrospectively delete this requirement or call the complete gate passed. The results support conditional competitive amplification of supplied inherited chemical traits; they do not show traits evolving greater fitness through repeated mutation and selection.

## Implications for the actual project

The research has moved beyond externally seeded copying: primitive artificial chemistry now generates its own catalytic roots, descendants and changed signatures in these four finite worlds. This is direct progress toward spontaneous origin, while retaining the distinction between autocatalysis and life.

Open-ended evolution remains structurally unaddressed by the assayed dimer carrier: four immutable material types yield only ten unordered pair signatures. This is a ceiling on the demonstrated carrier, not a theorem forbidding every other information-bearing structure in the general engine. Increasing the run duration alone will not expand that repertoire. A larger compositional carrier must demonstrate local copying, detachment, viable mutations and persistence under renewable resources before indefinite evolutionary novelty can be assessed.

There is no established learning organism, emergent intelligence, technology or civilization in these trajectories. Adding predefined agents would change the research objective rather than solve it.

## Evidence and limits

See PROTOCOL.md and protocol.json for thresholds fixed before execution; selection-result.json, origin-result.json and verification.json for results; each trajectory directory for immutable native checkpoints, events, birth records and replayable history. Source and executable hashes are retained. Seeds were selected prospectively but there is no held-out replication cohort. The worlds are small, horizons finite and initial excitation supplied. The selection and origin experiments have different conditions and cannot be pooled into one demonstration of spontaneous adaptive evolution.
