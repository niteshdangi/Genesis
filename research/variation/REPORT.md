# V3 material variation capability result

Four active v3 trajectories, four inactive-source v3 controls and four active v2 controls completed 2,048 ticks each and replayed. Both initial signatures and fresh seeds 41 and 42 were fixed before execution. All prospectively specified gates passed; no thresholds changed.

Active v3 trajectories produced 677 persistent dimers, including 312 immediate-parent signature changes and 279 faithful copies made by altered-signature parents. Maximum lineage depth per active trajectory was 10-12. All four inactive controls produced zero descendants. All four v2 controls produced descendants with zero signature changes. Energy and independent lineage-record checks passed throughout. The executable replayed historical v1 (10 checkpoint matches) and v2 (17 checkpoint matches) trajectories to their original digests.

Conclusion: the exact-signature restriction has been removed in opt-in v3. Changed dimers arise through accepted local catalytic events and can reproduce their own changed signatures. This is a designed artificial material capability, not spontaneous origin, competitive selection, adaptive evolution or a novel scientific breakthrough.

Limitations: initialized active chemistry, one designed template, disabled ordinary binding, breaking and switching; finite nonrenewed feedstock; ten unordered dimer signatures; only two fresh seeds with no held-out cohort. Birth-distance criteria are not complete physical isolation. Mutations are an explicitly permitted v3 recruitment error, not elemental transmutation. Equal affinities and catalytic strengths do not establish differential fitness. Higher v3 product counts than v2 must not be interpreted as adaptation.

The next scientific experiment is competition among inherited signatures sharing limited resources, with position-swapped and inactive controls and independently declared success criteria. This report does not claim that experiment has passed.

Source, executable hashes, prospective protocol, result.json, verification.json, checkpoint archives and catalytic birth records are preserved in this directory. Default laws remain v2; old v1/v2 simulations are not upgraded.
